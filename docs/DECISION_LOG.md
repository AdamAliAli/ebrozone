# Decision #001

## Title

The Website Tells One Story

### Status

✅ Approved

### Decision

The website is not a collection of pages.

It is one continuous story.

Every page and every section should guide students through the same emotional journey:

Fear → Safety → Hope → Growth → Confidence → Action

---

# Decision #002

## Title

The First Interaction Is With Ebro

### Status

✅ Approved

### Decision

Students should meet Ebro before they are introduced to the courses.

The homepage hero should create the feeling that Ebro is personally welcoming the visitor.

The first interaction should feel human, not commercial.

## Decision #010 — Build for Today's Needs

Version 1 should solve the problems Ebro has today, not the problems EbroZone may have in the future.

Features intended for larger teams, AI-assisted teaching, or operational scaling should only be introduced when they solve a real and existing need.

Whenever two solutions provide similar value, choose the simpler one.

---

## Decision #011 — A Document Is Complete When It Is Buildable

A planning document is considered complete when it provides enough clarity for an experienced developer to build the feature without making product decisions.

Perfection is not the goal.

Clarity is.

---

## Decision #012 — Accounts Are Created By Ebro, Not Self-Registered

Students do not create their own accounts.

Ebro creates a student's account after a consultation, once the student decides to enroll.

The platform then sends the student an activation email. The student becomes an active user by opening that email and setting their own password.

This keeps enrollment personal and curated, consistent with Ebro reviewing every consultation request before someone becomes a paying student, and avoids unattended account creation from the public website.

Activation links and password reset links share the same secure, expiring-link mechanism.

There is no public registration page.
