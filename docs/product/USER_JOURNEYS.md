# EbroZone User Journeys

> This document defines the primary user journeys within EbroZone Version 1.
>
> Every feature, page, and interaction should support one or more of these journeys while remaining aligned with the EbroZone Brand DNA.

---

# Journey 1 — Guest → Student

## Objective

Transform a first-time visitor into a confident EbroZone student by building trust before asking for commitment.

---

## Journey Flow

### Step 1 — Discover EbroZone

The visitor lands on the homepage.

They immediately feel welcomed through Ebro's introduction and the confidence-first message.

**Goal**

Create a strong first impression.

**Expected Emotion**

Safety.

---

### Step 2 — Build Trust

The visitor explores:

- About Ebro
- The Ebro Method
- Available Courses
- Student Success Stories

**Goal**

Show credibility and demonstrate that Ebro understands the learning journey.

**Expected Emotion**

Hope.

---

### Step 3 — Start the Journey

The visitor clicks:

**✨ Discover Your English Level**

**Goal**

Begin a personalized onboarding experience.

**Expected Emotion**

Curiosity.

---

### Step 4 — Personalized Onboarding

The onboarding experience includes:

- Welcome from Ebro
- Learning Goals
- English Level Assessment

**Goal**

Understand the student's needs before recommending a solution.

**Expected Emotion**

Confidence.

---

### Step 5 — Personalized Results

The visitor receives:

- English Level
- Personalized Feedback
- Recommended Course
- Suggested Learning Path

**Goal**

Provide immediate value before asking for any commitment.

**Expected Emotion**

Motivation.

---

### Step 6 — Book a Free Consultation

The visitor books a consultation with Ebro.

**Goal**

Begin a personal relationship.

**Expected Emotion**

Excitement.

---

### Step 7 — Consultation Preparation

Before the meeting, Ebro reviews:

- Assessment Results
- Student Goals
- Learning Recommendations

This allows every consultation to feel personal rather than generic.

---

### Step 8 — Become an EbroZone Student

The student decides to continue their learning journey with EbroZone.

↓

Payment Confirmation

↓

Ebro Creates the Student Account

↓

Activation Email Sent

↓

Student Sets Password (Account Activated)

↓

Student Dashboard Unlocked

↓

First Lesson Scheduled

**Journey Complete**

# Journey Principles

Every journey within EbroZone should follow these principles:

- Build trust before asking for commitment.
- Keep the number of steps to a minimum.
- Always provide a clear next action.
- Reduce friction wherever possible.
- Celebrate progress.
- Make every interaction feel human.
- Never leave users wondering what to do next.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful journey ensures that every user always knows:

- Where they are.
- Why they are there.
- What they should do next.
- What value they will receive.
- How they are progressing.

Every journey should reinforce the EbroZone promise:

**Helping students become confident English communicators through a safe, supportive, and personalized learning experience.**
