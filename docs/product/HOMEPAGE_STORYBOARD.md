# EbroZone Homepage Storyboard

> This document defines the complete storytelling experience of the EbroZone homepage.
>
> The homepage is not a collection of sections—it is one continuous story that guides visitors from uncertainty to confidence.
>
> Every section has one responsibility:
>
> **Move the visitor to the next emotion.**

---

# Emotional Journey

Every visitor should experience the following emotional progression:

Curiosity

↓

Safety

↓

"I've been understood."

↓

Hope

↓

Trust

↓

Possibility

↓

Action

Every section should naturally lead to the next without feeling disconnected.

---

# Section 1 — Hero

## Purpose

Capture attention immediately.

Welcome visitors personally.

Create a sense of safety before introducing any products or services.

---

## Visitor Emotion

"I'm in the right place."

---

## Story

Ebro looks directly at the visitor.

Instead of promoting courses, he welcomes them into the Ebro Zone.

Visitors should immediately feel accepted, supported, and understood.

---

## Main Message

**Welcome to the Ebro Zone.**

**You're safe here.**

**Let's build your confidence together.**

---

## Primary CTA

✨ Discover Your English Level

---

## Secondary CTA

▶ Watch My 60-Second Welcome

---

# Section 2 — We Understand You

## Purpose

Show visitors that we understand exactly what they are experiencing.

Build an emotional connection before offering any solution.

---

## Visitor Emotion

"This is exactly how I feel."

---

## Story

Talk about common struggles:

- Being afraid to speak English.
- Forgetting words while talking.
- Worrying about grammar mistakes.
- Feeling embarrassed.
- Losing confidence during conversations.
- Believing they are not good enough.

This section should focus entirely on empathy.

Do not introduce solutions yet.

---

# Section 3 — Imagine Your Future

## Purpose

Transform fear into hope.

Help visitors emotionally connect with the future they truly want.

---

## Visitor Emotion

"I want that future."

---

## Story

This section should feel cinematic and minimal.

Large typography.

Generous whitespace.

Soft transitions.

No distractions.

The experience should encourage visitors to pause and imagine themselves one year from now.

---

## Cinematic Experience

The following messages appear one after another through gentle fade transitions.

---

**Imagine yourself one year from today...**

↓

**Speaking English confidently.**

↓

**Walking into your interview without fear.**

↓

**Studying abroad with confidence.**

↓

**Traveling anywhere in the world.**

↓

**Meeting people from different cultures without hesitation.**

↓

A warm image of Ebro slowly appears.

---

## Closing Message

**That future starts with one conversation.**

---

## Primary CTA

✨ Discover Your English Level

---

## Design Principles

This section should create emotion through:

- Typography
- Photography
- Whitespace
- Timing
- Smooth transitions

Never rely on heavy animations or distracting visual effects.

The experience should feel premium because of its simplicity.

## Visitor Emotion

- The Ebro Method

# Section 5 — Meet Ebro

## Purpose

Build trust through authenticity.

---

## Visitor Emotion

"I trust him."

---

## Story

Present Ebro as more than an English teacher.

Share:

- His journey learning multiple languages.
- His teaching philosophy.
- His mission.
- His passion for helping students build confidence.

Visitors should feel that Ebro understands their journey because he has experienced language learning himself.
