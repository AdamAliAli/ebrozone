# EbroZone Booking Flow

> This document defines the complete consultation booking experience for EbroZone Version 1.

The booking process should feel personal, simple, and reassuring.

Students should never feel like they are filling out a complicated form.

Instead, they should feel like they are taking the first step toward achieving their goals.

---

# Booking Journey

## Step 1 — Personalized Recommendation

After completing the English Level Assessment, students receive:

- Their English level
- Personalized feedback
- Recommended course
- Suggested learning path

The page ends with:

**✨ Book Your Free Consultation**

---

## Step 2 — Choose Your Preferred Time

Students see Ebro's real-time availability.

Only available time slots can be selected.

Students choose:

- Date
- Time
- Time Zone (Automatically detected but editable)

The interface should be clean, visual, and easy to understand.

---

## Step 3 — Tell Us About Yourself

Before confirming the booking, students answer a few short questions.

Examples:

- What is your main goal?
- Have you studied English before?
- What do you find most difficult?
- Is there anything Ebro should know before meeting you?

This information helps Ebro prepare for a personalized consultation.

---

## Step 4 — Review & Confirm

Students review:

- Selected date
- Selected time
- Time zone
- Personal goals

Then they click:

**Confirm My Consultation**

---

## Step 5 — Booking Confirmation

A confirmation screen appears.

The student sees:

- Booking confirmed
- Consultation date
- Consultation time
- Time zone
- Meeting platform (Google Meet / Zoom)
- Next steps

The page should feel exciting and encouraging.

---

## Step 6 — Email Confirmation

Automatically send:

- Booking confirmation
- Calendar invitation
- Meeting link
- Consultation details
- Reschedule instructions

---

## Step 7 — Teacher Notification

Ebro receives:

- Student name
- Assessment results
- Learning goals
- Consultation date
- Consultation time

Everything needed to prepare before the meeting.

---

## Step 8 — Reminder System

Automatic reminders are sent before the consultation.

Suggested reminders:

- 24 hours before
- 1 hour before

---

# Booking Principles

The booking experience should always be:

- Simple
- Personal
- Fast
- Mobile-friendly
- Accessible
- Reassuring

Students should never wonder what happens next.

Every step should reduce uncertainty and increase confidence.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful booking flow ensures that students:

- Feel excited about their consultation.
- Know exactly when and how to join.
- Believe the consultation has been prepared specifically for them.
- Finish the booking process in less than three minutes.
