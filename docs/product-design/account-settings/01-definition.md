# Account Settings

Version: v1.0

Status: Approved for Design

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Account Settings module for EbroZone.

The Account Settings module allows authenticated users to manage their personal account information, security preferences, notifications, language, and application preferences from one centralized location.

---

# Business Goals

The Account Settings module should:

- Give users control over their accounts.
- Improve account security.
- Reduce administrative requests.
- Support personalization.
- Scale with future platform features.

---

# User Experience Goals

The module should feel:

- Personal
- Secure
- Simple
- Reliable
- Easy to navigate

Users should always feel in control of their account.

---

# Module Scope

Version 1 includes:

- Profile Information
- Password Management
- Notification Preferences
- Language Preferences
- Appearance Preferences

Future versions may include:

- Two-Factor Authentication
- Connected Accounts
- Privacy Controls
- Session Management

---

# Design Principles

The module should:

- Organize settings into logical sections.
- Minimize unnecessary steps.
- Clearly communicate successful updates.
- Keep security-related actions highly visible.
- Follow the EbroZone Design System.

---

# Success Criteria

The Account Settings module is successful when:

- Users can easily update their information.
- Security settings are easy to manage.
- Personal preferences are simple to customize.
- The module supports future expansion.
