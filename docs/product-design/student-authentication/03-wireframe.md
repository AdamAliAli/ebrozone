# Student Authentication Wireframe

Version: v1.0

Status: Approved for Design

---

# Purpose

This document defines the structural layout of the Student Authentication page.

The layout should be simple, focused, and distraction-free, allowing students to sign in quickly.

---

# Page Structure

Navigation

↓

Login Section

↓

Forgot Password Link

↓

Help Section

↓

Footer

---

# Layout Principles

The page should:

- Keep the login form as the visual focus.
- Minimize distractions.
- Require minimal scrolling.
- Feel calm and professional.

---

# Success Criteria

The wireframe is successful when:

- Students immediately understand how to sign in.
- The login form is the primary focus.
- The layout is clean and easy to scan.
