# Student Authentication High-Fidelity Layout

Version: v1.0

Status: Approved for UI Design

---

# Purpose

This document defines the final visual layout of the Student Authentication page.

The page should feel secure, professional, and welcoming while allowing students to access their learning platform with minimal effort.

---

# Overall Design Direction

The page should feel:

- Clean
- Modern
- Professional
- Secure
- Calm

The login form should remain the primary visual focus.

---

# Layout

Desktop

Split layout:

- Left: Welcome content and branding
- Right: Login form

Tablet

Centered login card with reduced spacing.

Mobile

Single-column layout with the login form taking priority.

---

# Welcome Section

Contains:

- Welcome headline
- Short supporting message
- EbroZone branding
- Optional illustration or teacher image

Purpose:

Reassure students that they are entering the learning platform.

---

# Login Form

Contains:

- Email field
- Password field
- Remember Me checkbox
- Sign In button
- Forgot Password link

The form should appear inside a clean card with generous spacing.

---

# Help Section

Display a short message explaining how students can contact support if they cannot access their account.

---

# Footer

Use the standard platform footer with:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Contact Information

---

# Layout Principles

The layout should:

- Keep the login form above the fold.
- Minimize distractions.
- Reinforce trust through simplicity.
- Follow the Design System.

---

# Success Criteria

The layout is successful when:

- Students immediately recognize where to sign in.
- The login process feels simple.
- The design reflects the EbroZone brand.
- The page is ready for implementation.
