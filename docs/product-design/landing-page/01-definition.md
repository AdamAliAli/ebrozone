# Purpose

This document defines the structure, objectives, and user experience of the EbroZone landing page.

The landing page is designed to build trust, communicate value, reduce uncertainty, and guide visitors toward booking a free consultation.

Every section should answer a specific question in the visitor's mind while creating a smooth journey from curiosity to conversion.

---

# Business Goals

The landing page should:

- Build trust immediately.
- Introduce the EbroZone philosophy.
- Present Ebro as a trusted English mentor.
- Explain the learning journey.
- Demonstrate real student success.
- Encourage visitors to book a free consultation.

The landing page exists to convert visitors into consultation bookings.

---

# User Experience Goals

Visitors should feel:

Curious

↓

Interested

↓

Comfortable

↓

Confident

↓

Motivated

↓

Ready to Book

Every section should reduce uncertainty and increase confidence.

---

# Design Principles

The landing page should:

- Tell a story rather than present isolated sections.
- Use generous whitespace.
- Maintain a premium visual hierarchy.
- Keep content easy to scan.
- Focus on one primary CTA.
- Feel modern, human, and trustworthy.

The page should never feel overwhelming.

---

# Visual Direction

The landing page should reflect a premium English academy rather than a traditional online course platform.

Visual guidelines:

- Professional teacher photography.
- Authentic student moments.
- Soft gradients in marketing sections.
- Rounded modern components.
- Large whitespace.
- Clean typography.
- Purposeful animations.

The teacher should remain the visual center of the brand.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful landing page ensures that visitors:

- Understand EbroZone.
- Trust the teacher.
- Understand the learning process.
- Feel emotionally connected.
- Book a free consultation confidently.
