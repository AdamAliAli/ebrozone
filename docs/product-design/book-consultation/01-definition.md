# Book Consultation

Version: v1.0

Status: Approved for Design

---

# Purpose

This document defines the consultation booking experience for EbroZone.

The goal is to make booking a consultation simple, welcoming, and frictionless while collecting enough information for Ebro to contact the visitor.

Visitors should not be required to create an account before requesting a consultation.

---

# Business Goals

The consultation page should:

- Maximize consultation requests.
- Minimize form abandonment.
- Create a professional first impression.
- Collect the information needed for follow-up.
- Convert visitors into enrolled students.

---

# User Experience Goals

The booking experience should feel:

- Simple
- Fast
- Friendly
- Professional
- Personal

The form should take less than two minutes to complete.

---

# Required Information

Required:

- Full Name
- Phone Number

Optional:

- Email Address
- Preferred Contact Time
- Current English Level
- Learning Goal
- Additional Notes

---

# Booking Flow

Visitor

↓

Completes Consultation Form

↓

Submits Request

↓

Receives Confirmation

↓

Ebro Reviews Request

↓

Ebro Contacts Visitor

↓

Consultation

↓

Visitor Decides to Enroll

↓

Student Account Created

---

# Design Principles

The page should:

- Reduce friction.
- Explain what happens next.
- Build confidence.
- Keep the form short.
- Encourage completion.

---

# Success Criteria

The consultation page is successful when:

- Visitors complete the form easily.
- Visitors understand the next step.
- Ebro receives enough information to start the conversation.
- More visitors become students.
