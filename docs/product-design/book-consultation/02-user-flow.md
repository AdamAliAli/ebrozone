# Book Consultation User Flow

Version: v1.0

Status: Approved for Design

---

# Purpose

This document defines how visitors move through the consultation booking experience.

The objective is to make requesting a consultation feel effortless while preparing the visitor for the next step.

---

# Primary Goal

Convert interested visitors into consultation requests.

---

# Primary User Flow

Visitor

↓

Clicks "Book Free Consultation"

↓

Arrives at Consultation Page

↓

Reads a short introduction

↓

Completes the consultation form

↓

Submits the request

↓

Receives confirmation

↓

Waits to be contacted by Ebro

↓

Attends the consultation

↓

Decides whether to enroll

---

# Alternative Flow

Visitor

↓

Starts filling the form

↓

Leaves before submitting

↓

Returns later

↓

Completes the request

---

# Decision Points

The visitor can:

- Submit the consultation request.
- Return to the Landing Page.
- Explore Courses before deciding.
- Leave the website.

---

# User Questions

The page should answer:

- Is this free?
- What happens after I submit?
- How will Ebro contact me?
- How long will it take to receive a response?

These answers should appear naturally throughout the page.

---

# Friction Points

Potential concerns include:

- Sharing a phone number.
- Uncertainty about the next step.
- Fear of being pressured into buying.

The page should reduce these concerns before submission.

---

# Success Criteria

The user flow is successful when:

- Visitors complete the form confidently.
- Visitors understand what happens next.
- The booking process feels simple and trustworthy.
