# Book Consultation Wireframe

Version: v1.0

Status: Approved for Design

---

# Purpose

This document defines the structural layout of the Book Consultation page.

The focus is on organizing information in a way that makes booking feel simple, reassuring, and fast.

---

# Layout Principles

The page should:

- Keep the form as the primary focus.
- Explain each step clearly.
- Minimize scrolling.
- Build trust before asking for personal information.

---

# Success Criteria

The wireframe is successful when:

- Visitors immediately understand the page.
- The consultation form is easy to find.
- The booking process feels short and approachable.
