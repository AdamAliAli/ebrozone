# Purpose

This document defines the structural layout of the English Assessment.

The assessment should be easy to complete, visually engaging, and focused on helping visitors understand their English level.

# Layout Principles

The assessment should:

- Display one question at a time.
- Minimize distractions.
- Clearly communicate progress.
- Keep the next action obvious.

---

# Success Criteria

The wireframe is successful when:

- Visitors understand where they are in the assessment.
- Questions are easy to answer.
- Results are easy to understand.
- The consultation CTA feels like a natural next step.
