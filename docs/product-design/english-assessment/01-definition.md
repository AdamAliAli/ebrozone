# English Assessment

Version: v1.0

Status: Approved for Design

---

# Purpose

This document defines the English Assessment experience for EbroZone.

The assessment helps prospective students understand their current English level and provides Ebro with useful information before the consultation.

The assessment is optional and does not require an account.

---

# Business Goals

The English Assessment should:

- Encourage visitor engagement.
- Help visitors understand their current level.
- Provide useful information before the consultation.
- Support personalized learning recommendations.
- Increase consultation bookings.

---

# User Experience Goals

The assessment should feel:

- Simple
- Encouraging
- Interactive
- Accurate
- Low pressure

Visitors should feel that the assessment is helping them rather than testing them.

---

# Assessment Objectives

The assessment should:

- Estimate the visitor's English level.
- Identify strengths and weaknesses.
- Recommend the next step.
- Encourage booking a consultation.

The result is a recommendation, not an official certification.

---

# Assessment Scope

Version 1 includes:

- Speaking Confidence
- Grammar
- Vocabulary
- Reading
- Listening

Writing can be added in a future version.

---

# Design Principles

The assessment should:

- Be easy to complete.
- Keep questions short.
- Show clear progress.
- Reduce test anxiety.
- Encourage completion.

---

# Success Criteria

The assessment is successful when:

- Visitors complete it confidently.
- Results feel helpful and understandable.
- Visitors are encouraged to book a consultation.
- Ebro receives useful information for the consultation.
