Version: v1.0

Status: Approved for Design

# Purpose

This document defines the structural layout of the Teacher Dashboard.

The layout should help teachers quickly understand their daily responsibilities and access teaching tools with minimal navigation.

# Layout Principles

The dashboard should:

- Prioritize urgent tasks.
- Keep navigation consistent.
- Minimize unnecessary clicks.
- Present information in a logical order.

---

# Success Criteria

The wireframe is successful when:

- Teachers immediately understand their priorities.
- Daily teaching activities are easy to access.
- The dashboard feels organized and efficient.
