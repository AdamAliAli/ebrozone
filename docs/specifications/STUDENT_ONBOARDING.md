# Student Onboarding Specification

> This document defines the onboarding experience for new students in EbroZone Version 1.

The onboarding experience should feel welcoming, simple, and encouraging.

Students should be able to join EbroZone in less than one minute and then choose the path that best fits their needs.

The platform should guide students, never force them.

---

# Goals

The onboarding should:

- Welcome new students.
- Build trust.
- Create a frictionless registration experience.
- Personalize the learning journey.
- Allow students to choose their preferred next step.
- Encourage long-term engagement with EbroZone.

---

# Entry Points

Students can enter EbroZone through one of three paths:

- 🚀 Start Learning
- 📊 Discover Your English Level
- 📚 Explore Courses

Regardless of the entry point, registration remains fast and simple.

---

# Step 1 — Create a Free Account

Students create their free EbroZone account.

Required information:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Password

Future versions may support:

- Google Sign-In
- Apple Sign-In

The registration process should take less than one minute.

---

# Step 2 — Welcome

After registration, students are welcomed into EbroZone.

Example:

> **Welcome to the Ebro Zone.**
>
> **You're safe here.**
>
> **Let's build your confidence together.**

Students should immediately feel supported rather than overwhelmed.

---

# Step 3 — Choose Your Next Step

Students choose how they would like to begin their journey.

### 📊 Discover Your English Level (Recommended)

Take a short assessment and receive:

- Estimated English Level
- Personalized Feedback
- Course Recommendation

---

### 👨‍🏫 Book a Free Consultation

Meet Ebro personally to:

- Discuss your goals
- Receive personalized advice
- Ask questions
- Build your learning plan

---

### 📚 Explore Courses

Browse available courses before making any decisions.

Students are free to return and complete the assessment or book a consultation at any time.

Nothing is mandatory.

---

# Step 4 — Student Dashboard

After registration, students immediately gain access to their dashboard.

The dashboard includes:

- Profile
- Upcoming Bookings
- Messages
- Your Confidence Journey
- Assessment Results (if completed)
- Recommended Course (if available)

Students can complete any unfinished onboarding steps whenever they choose.

---

# Step 5 — Welcome Email

Automatically send a welcome email containing:

- Welcome Message
- Dashboard Link
- Helpful Resources
- Next Steps

If the student books a consultation, send an additional email containing:

- Consultation Details
- Meeting Link
- Calendar Invitation

---

# User Experience Rules

The onboarding experience should always feel:

- Friendly
- Fast
- Encouraging
- Personal
- Simple

Students should never feel pressured to complete every step immediately.

They should always understand what they can do next.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful onboarding experience ensures that students:

- Complete registration in less than one minute.
- Feel welcomed into the EbroZone community.
- Understand the available learning paths.
- Begin exploring the platform with confidence.
- Return to continue their learning journey, whether through an assessment, consultation, or course enrollment.
