# Student Onboarding Specification

> This document defines the onboarding experience for new students in EbroZone Version 1.

The onboarding experience should feel welcoming, simple, and encouraging.

Visitors should be able to explore EbroZone in less than one minute, with no account required, and then choose the path that best fits their needs.

The platform should guide students, never force them.

---

# Goals

The onboarding should:

- Welcome new visitors.
- Build trust.
- Create a frictionless, account-free exploration experience.
- Personalize the learning journey.
- Allow visitors to choose their preferred next step.
- Encourage long-term engagement with EbroZone.

---

# Entry Points

Visitors can enter EbroZone through one of three paths:

- 🚀 Start Learning
- 📊 Discover Your English Level
- 📚 Explore Courses

Regardless of the entry point, no account is required to begin.

---

# Step 1 — Welcome

Visitors are welcomed into EbroZone.

Example:

> **Welcome to the Ebro Zone.**
>
> **You're safe here.**
>
> **Let's build your confidence together.**

Visitors should immediately feel supported rather than overwhelmed.

---

# Step 2 — Choose Your Next Step

Visitors choose how they would like to begin their journey.

### 📊 Discover Your English Level (Recommended)

Take a short assessment and receive:

- Estimated English Level
- Personalized Feedback
- Course Recommendation

---

### 👨‍🏫 Book a Free Consultation

Meet Ebro personally to:

- Discuss your goals
- Receive personalized advice
- Ask questions
- Build your learning plan

No account is required to book a consultation.

---

### 📚 Explore Courses

Browse available courses before making any decisions.

Visitors are free to return and complete the assessment or book a consultation at any time.

Nothing is mandatory.

---

# Step 3 — Enrollment

If the visitor decides to enroll after their consultation, Ebro creates their student account.

The platform then sends an activation email.

The visitor becomes a student by:

- Opening the activation email
- Setting their own password

See `docs/product-design/student-authentication/01-definition.md` for the full account activation flow.

---

# Step 4 — Student Dashboard

Once the account is activated, students gain access to their dashboard.

The dashboard includes:

- Profile
- Upcoming Bookings
- Messages
- Your Confidence Journey
- Assessment Results (if completed)
- Recommended Course (if available)

---

# Step 5 — Welcome Email

The welcome message is delivered as part of the account activation email, and includes:

- Welcome Message
- Activation Link
- Helpful Resources
- Next Steps

If the visitor books a consultation before enrolling, send a separate email containing:

- Consultation Details
- Meeting Link
- Calendar Invitation

---

# User Experience Rules

The onboarding experience should always feel:

- Friendly
- Fast
- Encouraging
- Personal
- Simple

Visitors should never feel pressured to complete every step immediately.

They should always understand what they can do next.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful onboarding experience ensures that:

- Visitors can explore EbroZone without creating an account.
- Visitors feel welcomed into the EbroZone community.
- Visitors understand the available learning paths.
- Visitors begin exploring the platform with confidence.
- Enrolled students can activate their account in less than one minute.
