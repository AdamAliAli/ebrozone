# English Level Assessment Specification

> This document defines how the English Level Assessment works in EbroZone Version 1.

The assessment is designed to estimate the student's current English level and recommend the most suitable learning path.

It is **not** an official certification.

Its purpose is to personalize the student's journey.

---

# Goals

The assessment should:

- Welcome new visitors.
- Reduce uncertainty.
- Personalize recommendations.
- Increase consultation bookings.
- Build trust before asking for payment.

---

# Estimated Duration

5–8 minutes

Students should always see their progress.

Example:

Question 6 of 20

---

# Assessment Structure

## Section 1 — Personal Goals

Collect:

- Why are you learning English?
- Your biggest challenge
- Your preferred learning style

Purpose:

Personalize the recommendation.

---

## Section 2 — Grammar

Multiple-choice questions.

Evaluate:

- Tenses
- Articles
- Prepositions
- Sentence structure

---

## Section 3 — Vocabulary

Multiple-choice questions.

Evaluate:

- Everyday vocabulary
- Context understanding
- Word usage

---

## Section 4 — Reading

Short paragraph.

3–5 comprehension questions.

---

## Section 5 — Listening (Optional)

Version 1:

Not included.

Future Version:

Short audio clips with comprehension questions.

---

## Section 6 — Speaking (Optional)

Version 1:

Not included.

Future Version:

AI-assisted speaking assessment.

---

# Question Types

Supported:

- Multiple Choice
- Fill in the Blank
- Reading Comprehension

Version 1 intentionally avoids open-ended questions.

---

# Number of Questions

Approximately:

20 Questions

Distribution:

- Grammar — 8
- Vocabulary — 7
- Reading — 5

---

# Result Levels

Map results to CEFR:

- A1
- A2
- B1
- B2
- C1
- C2

The assessment provides an estimate only.

# User Experience Rules

Students should never feel like they are taking an exam.

The experience should feel encouraging.

Avoid language such as:

❌ Wrong

❌ Failed

❌ Poor

Instead use:

✓ Good foundation

✓ Room for improvement

✓ Great potential

✓ Recommended next step

---

# Data Stored

Save:

- Answers
- Estimated Level
- Learning Goal
- Recommended Course
- Completion Date

The assessment history may be shown in future versions.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful assessment should:

- Feel friendly.
- Take less than 8 minutes.
- Produce useful recommendations.
- Increase consultation bookings.
- Leave students feeling hopeful and motivated.
